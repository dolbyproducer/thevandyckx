About

The Van Dyckx

Please welcome The Van Dyckx. Intelligent indie pop/rock with an ounce of wit. Exercises in poetry. Music that doesn't self-impose the generally known industry boundaries and standards. Songs that tell stories. Songs about love. About anger. Or the girl from next door. Everything that surrounds us and makes us think and tick. Well, The Van Dyckx at least.

Discover the world through the eyes of The Van Dyckx. If you feel like it, do a little silly dance to one of their songs. If you don't, you might want to smash some public property.

Or not. It's up to you. Just don't take it all too seriously.

I know we don't.
